Your Inquiries Answered

How Is The Required Scope Defined?

Commissions are accepted exclusively for comprehensive spatial renovations or ground-up construction. Single-room curation or isolated furnishing requests are not currently accommodated.

Are International Commissions Accepted?

Global projects are executed through an established network of vetted fabrication partners. Site oversight and architectural logistics are coordinated directly by the principal designers.

What Is The Standard Project Timeline?

The realization of a commission typically spans eighteen to thirty-six months. Timelines are strictly dictated by structural complexity and the procurement of rare antiquities.

Are New Commissions Currently Accepted?

A fixed architectural retainer is established upon the initiation of a dialogue. Fabrication, procurement, and logistics are billed transparently throughout the phases of engagement.

How Are Custom Elements Sourced?

Architectural antiquities are globally procured through private gallery networks. Bespoke millwork and monolithic furnishings are engineered site-specifically to ensure absolute integration with the final spatial envelope.
